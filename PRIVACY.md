# AppRinse Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

AppRinse is a native macOS utility for reviewing and removing applications and related files. This privacy policy explains what AppRinse does with information when you use the app.

## Information AppRinse processes

AppRinse processes local information on your Mac, including application bundles, file paths, file sizes, and local cleanup preferences. This information is used to display installed applications, identify related files, and carry out actions you request. It is not sent to AppRinse or stored on a remote server.

AppRinse does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, payment information, precise location, contacts, photos, or user-generated content. AppRinse does not use advertising identifiers, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics for its own services.

## Network connections

When you use optional update, package, or application-discovery features, AppRinse may connect to public services such as GitHub, Homebrew, or other software distribution endpoints. These services may receive standard technical request information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. AppRinse does not send your local file contents or cleanup history to these services.

## Local storage and deletion

AppRinse may store preferences and undo-history data locally so that the app can remember your choices and support recovery workflows. You can remove this local data by deleting AppRinse's application-support data or uninstalling the app.

## Privileged actions

Some cleanup operations require macOS authorization. AppRinse uses Apple's authorization mechanisms and only performs privileged file operations that you explicitly request.
